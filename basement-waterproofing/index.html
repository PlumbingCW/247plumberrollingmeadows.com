Where the Water Comes From

Water enters a basement in a few main ways: through cracks or the joint where the wall meets the floor, through floor drains that back up, through window wells, and through groundwater rising under the slab. Each has a different fix, and treating the wrong one wastes money.

The plumber looks at when the water appears. Water that arrives only during heavy rain points to groundwater or surface drainage. Water with sewage smell points to a sewer line problem. Water that appears without rain may be a leaking pipe, which the leak detection page covers.

Local Water Context

Rolling Meadows is a flat-to-gently-rolling city along Salt Creek, and the city manages more than 54 miles of storm sewer, with Plum Grove Reservoir and Twin Lakes Reservoir helping to control heavy rain. Its storm water guidance tells residents to expect up to 48 hours for standing water to drain before calling it a storm sewer problem.

Because much of the housing dates from 1950 to 1980, many basements have older walls and floor slabs that have settled and cracked over the decades. We do not guess at your foundation. The plumber looks at the actual cracks, drains and pit and explains what he sees before you decide.

Low-Cost Steps Before Major Work

  • Clean gutters and extend downspouts several feet from the foundation
  • Check that the ground slopes away from the house
  • Test the sump pump by pouring water into the pit
  • Keep window wells clear and covered
  • Store valuables off the floor and away from the walls

Interior Versus Exterior Work

Exterior waterproofing means digging around the foundation, which is a larger job that involves excavation. Interior systems collect water that reaches the wall and floor and send it to a sump. Interior systems are more common in finished homes and are usually the plumber's part of the job.

Some problems are best handled by a foundation or masonry specialist, such as large structural cracks. We will say so if that is the case, so you are not paying for plumbing work that will not solve the problem.
