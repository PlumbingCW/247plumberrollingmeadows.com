When No Hot Water Is an Emergency

A heater that has quit is uncomfortable, but a leaking tank is urgent. Water on the floor around the base can mean a bad drain valve, a loose fitting or a failed tank, and a failed tank can release its whole volume quickly. Shut off the cold water to the heater, switch off the power or gas, and keep belongings off the floor.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility before you call a plumber. Otherwise, call the 24/7 line, and dispatch will explain whether a same-day appointment is open and whether a repair is likely to be worth it.

Repair or Replace: How We Help You Decide

A repair makes sense when the tank is sound and the failed part is small, such as a heating element, thermostat, igniter or thermocouple. A tank that is leaking from the shell cannot be repaired, and a unit that is already many years old may not be worth a major part.

The plumber looks at the age printed on the data label, the condition of the tank and the cost of the repair, then gives you a clear price for both paths. If replacement is the better value, our water heater replacement page walks through what that involves, and you make the call.

Common Repairs on Gas Water Heaters

On gas units the repairs we see most are a failed thermocouple or flame sensor, a dirty burner or pilot orifice, a gas control valve that has failed and a draft or venting problem. Venting matters, because exhaust that does not leave the house safely is a carbon monoxide risk.

If the pilot will not stay lit after a relight, do not keep trying. Repeated relight attempts with a faulty sensor can let gas collect. A plumber can test the sensor and gas valve and tell you what failed.

Common Repairs on Electric Water Heaters

Electric tanks have two heating elements and two thermostats, and one can fail while the other keeps working. That is why water often turns lukewarm instead of cold. A plumber tests each element and thermostat with a meter, then replaces only what failed.

A tripped breaker or a high-limit reset button can also cut the power. Resetting either once is reasonable, but if it trips again, there is an underlying fault and you should not keep resetting it.

Tankless Heaters and Hybrid Units

Tankless units fail differently from tanks. The common problems are scale on the heat exchanger, a clogged inlet filter, ignition faults and error codes. Many of those respond to a descaling flush, which is why the tankless water heater page has a section on flush and descale service.

If your unit is a heat pump or hybrid model, tell dispatch so the plumber arrives with the right parts and diagnostic approach.

Water Heaters in Rolling Meadows Homes

Rolling Meadows water comes from Lake Michigan through the city's Public Works system, and the city's water quality report does not publish a hardness figure. That is why we do not quote one here. Sediment collects in any tank over time, and a plumber can show you how much has settled in yours.

The city requires a permit for a new or replacement water heater, which applies to replacements and not to most repairs. If a repair turns into a replacement, the permit step is part of that job, and the contractor's license and bond must be on file with Community Development.
