Rolling Meadows, ZIP 60008

Rolling Meadows sits in Cook County about 24 miles northwest of the Chicago Loop, in parts of Palatine, Elk Grove and Wheeling townships. Our dispatch address on Algonquin Road is in ZIP 60008, the one ZIP code that covers the city.

Calls come from the whole city. That includes the Kimball Hill-era neighborhoods near the Kirchoff Road corridor, the Plum Grove area, apartments and offices near Golf Road and Algonquin Road, and homes close to Salt Creek, where wet basements are a recurring concern. If you are in the city and you need an emergency drain cleaning or a water heater repair, the call goes straight to dispatch.

Palatine Has Its Own Page

Palatine is adjacent to Rolling Meadows but has its own village hall, its own permit office and a different water supplier. The page for emergency plumber service in Palatine covers the ZIP codes, the village's water heater permit steps and the creek watersheds that affect basements there.

Neighborhood Pages in Rolling Meadows

Three parts of the city have their own pages: Kimball Hill, the Plum Grove area and the Kirchoff Road corridor. Each covers what local sources say about the area, with no claim of an official boundary.

Other Towns We Serve From Rolling Meadows

These nearby communities do not have a separate page yet, but the same 24/7 line covers them. The ZIP codes are the nearby postal codes we serve, not a statement about who staffs the call.

  • Arlington Heights, ZIP 60004 and 60005, an adjacent village that shares a park district with parts of Rolling Meadows
  • Schaumburg, ZIP 60173, 60193, 60194 and 60195, the large adjacent village
  • Elk Grove Village, ZIP 60007, an adjacent village with a mix of industrial and residential blocks
  • Mount Prospect, ZIP 60056, a nearby village with an older housing stock
  • Hoffman Estates and Prospect Heights, covered on request from the same dispatch

How We Decide What Is Covered

We tell you honestly if an address is too far for a realistic emergency response. If you are unsure, call and give the street and nearest cross street. Dispatch will tell you whether we can get there and when appointments are open, and you decide with that information.

Service Area Questions
