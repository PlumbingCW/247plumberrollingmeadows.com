What the Kirchoff Road Corridor Is

The City of Rolling Meadows treats the Kirchoff Road Corridor as a planning district. The 2018 comprehensive plan calls it the center of town and lists it among the city's key sub-areas. It is a mixed corridor, not a single neighborhood, so this page covers both businesses and residents.

The Rolling Meadows Shopping Center on Kirchoff Road was announced in April 1955 with thirteen original tenants, according to the Daily Herald. Kimball Hill Park sits at 3266 Kirchoff Road and Rotary Park is on Kirchoff near Route 53. Daily Herald headlines from 2023 and 2024 report a city hold on redevelopment along the road, and a corridor study was completed in February 2025.

Condominiums and Older Buildings Along the Road

Real estate listing data places two condominium communities on Kirchoff Road. Park Meadows sits on the south side east of Route 53 and shows build years of 1976 to 1980. Kirchoff Meadows is at 3255 and 3265 Kirchoff Road and shows build years of 1988 to 1998. That data comes from a listing site and is supporting evidence only.

In multi-unit buildings, the water heater, shutoff and drain lines are often shared in some way. Before you hire anyone, find out from your board or manager which parts you own and which the association maintains.

Business Plumbing: Planning Around Shared Lines

Commercial buildings often have several tenants on one sewer line. A clog that shows up in your unit may begin in another, which is why a camera look can save repeat visits. Ask the landlord who is responsible for the building drain before a clearing starts.

Kitchens and food counters add grease to the mix. The commercial plumbing page covers after-hours calls and shared drain lines. We do not claim any specific tenant or customer along the road.

Who Maintains the Sewer Between the Building and the Main

The City of Rolling Meadows owns the sewer main. Its City of Rolling Meadows Sanitary Sewer Problem Guide says the owner is responsible for the private lateral from the building to the main, and that Public Works will check the main line at no cost when a sewer backs up.

Most sewer work needs a city permit, so check with the Community Development office before any excavation. For a backup, call Public Works at 847-963-0500, then call us if the main is clear.
