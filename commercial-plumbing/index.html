Commercial Plumbing Emergency

Shut off water at the main or at the zone valve if you know where it is, and tell staff or tenants to avoid the area. If a restaurant or shop floor drain is backing up, stop dishwashing and restroom use. If the water is near electrical gear, keep people away.

Call dispatch with the address, the type of building, the problem and a contact who can approve the price. Many commercial calls need approval from a manager or owner, and having that contact ready saves time.

Who Calls Us in Rolling Meadows

Rolling Meadows has several business districts. The Golf and Algonquin Road areas have office towers along I-90, the Plum Grove Road corridors have commercial centers and the Northwest Industrial District hosts light industry and warehouses. The city's plan notes that multifamily buildings of three or four stories make up part of its housing. The central road has its own page for plumbers on the Kirchoff Road corridor.

That mix means calls from offices with restroom clogs, small shops with water heater trouble, restaurants with grease line backups and apartment buildings with shared stack or boiler problems. Each has a different set of fixtures and a different clock, and the plumber plans accordingly.

Owners, Managers and Tenants

In leased space, who pays depends on the lease and on whether the problem is in the tenant space or the building system. We can talk with the property manager if you give us the contact, and we write the cause on the invoice so the responsibility is clear.

The city notes that blockages in the right of way are the city's to clear, but the sewer lateral from the building to the main is the owner's. If you manage a property, find out where your lateral and cleanout are before an emergency. The sewer inspection page explains how a camera finds the problem.

Sources and Local Rules Behind This Page

Plumbing work in Illinois is governed by the Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890, which municipal inspectors enforce, and the Illinois Department of Public Health licenses plumbers. The ownership and cleanout guidance here comes from the City of Rolling Meadows sewer guide, according to which the owner maintains the lateral to the main.

We name these sources so you can check them yourself. Rules and phone numbers change, so confirm with the city office or the state before you rely on any detail for a permit or a purchase.
