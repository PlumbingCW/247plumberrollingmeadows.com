What to Tell Dispatch

A few details let us send the right tools on the first trip.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street
  • Where the water or sewage is and how fast it is coming
  • Whether you have found and closed the main shutoff
  • What the fixture, heater or drain does now compared with normal
  • Whether you own or rent, and whether a landlord or manager needs to approve work

Before You Call About a Sewer Backup

The City of Rolling Meadows asks residents with a sewer backup to call Public Works at 847-963-0500 before calling a plumber, because a blockage in the city main is the city's to clear. After hours, the city lists its non-emergency police line at 847-255-2416. If the city confirms the main is clear, call us and we will handle the private lateral.
