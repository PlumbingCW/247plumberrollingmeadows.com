How to Pick the Right Service Page

Most people do not search for a service by its trade name. They search for what they can see, like a toilet that will not stop running, a wet spot on the ceiling or a water heater that makes noise. This page works backward from the symptom so you land on the right service quickly.

If more than one row fits, start with the one that involves water you cannot stop. Water that keeps running is an emergency burst pipe repair call. A drain that will not clear is an emergency drain cleaning call. Anything else is usually a same-day appointment when one is available.

What Is Different About Plumbing in Rolling Meadows

Most of the housing here was built between 1950 and 1980, according to the city's comprehensive plan, so many calls involve aging supply lines, original drain stacks and water heaters that have already been replaced once or twice. Pages on repiping, leak detection and sewer inspection spend extra time on that reality.

The city also splits responsibility in a way that surprises people. Rolling Meadows owns the water service line up to the shutoff valve, the homeowner owns it from the valve into the house, and the homeowner owns the sewer lateral out to the city main. Knowing that before a call can save a wasted visit.
