Emergency Plumbing in Palatine: What To Do First

Palatine homeowners can reach our 24/7 emergency line the same way as Rolling Meadows customers. Close the main water valve for a burst pipe, stop running drains when sewage is backing up and keep out of standing water near outlets. Then call with the address and the symptom.

The village says an emergency water heater replacement may start without a permit if the permit is applied for within one business day, and a final inspection is required. Mention it when you call if your water heater has failed, so the plumber can plan the paperwork.

Before the Plumber Arrives in Palatine

A few quick steps limit the damage while you wait.

Find the main shutoff

It is usually where the water line enters, near the meter. Turn it clockwise.

Stop using drains

If a drain is backing up, hold off on sinks, showers and laundry.

Protect the electrics

Stay out of standing water near panels and outlets.

Photograph the damage

Insurers want to see what the water did before cleanup.

A Little About Palatine

Palatine is a village in Cook County, with a small part in Lake County, founded in 1866 and home to 67,908 people at the 2020 census. It sits in Palatine and Ela townships and has a Metra Union Pacific Northwest station. Schools are served by districts 15 and 211.

Its water comes from the Northwest Water Commission, which supplies Lake Michigan water through Evanston. That is a different supplier than Rolling Meadows uses, so water quality questions in Palatine go to the village. Public Works can be reached at 847-705-5200.

Permits and Inspections in Palatine

The Village of Palatine runs permits through Community Development at 200 E Wood St, and its Express permit program handles some projects online. A water heater permit needs a final inspection, and the village sets rules for installations, such as a gas water heater in a garage being raised 18 inches off the floor.

For water heaters in confined spaces the village requires combustion air openings of 100 square inches within 12 inches of the top and bottom of the enclosure. If your closet is tight, ask the plumber about ventilation before the replacement. See water heater replacement for the full process.

Water, Streams and Storm Season

Palatine's streams drain toward Salt Creek, which rises at Wilke Marsh, and the northeast side of the village drains to the Buffalo Creek watershed. Heavy rain therefore stresses both older storm sewers and household sump pumps.

If your basement takes on water during storms, the sump pump repair page explains the checks a plumber runs. A pump that cycles constantly or struggles in a storm is worth testing before the next one.

ZIP Codes We Serve in Palatine

The main Palatine ZIP codes for street addresses are 60067 and 60074, both shown above. Other Palatine-area codes include 60038 and 60055, which are unique codes tied to specific organizations, and 60078, 60094 and 60095, which are PO box codes and not service addresses.

Tell dispatch your street address and ZIP, and they will confirm whether the plumber can reach you. If your address is in a neighboring town, we also dispatch to Arlington Heights, Schaumburg, Elk Grove Village and Mount Prospect from Rolling Meadows.

Sources and Local Rules Behind This Page
