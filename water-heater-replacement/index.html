A Failed Tank: What to Do Right Now

A tank that is leaking from the shell has failed, and no repair fixes that. Close the cold water inlet valve on top of the heater, switch off the breaker or turn the gas valve to off, and, if water is spreading, open a hot tap upstairs to relieve pressure. Move anything stored near the heater.

Take a photo of the leak and the data label before cleanup. It helps your insurance claim and tells the plumber the tank size and fuel type, which can speed up the visit.

How a Replacement Visit Goes

The plumber confirms the size, fuel type, venting and fittings, then drains and removes the old unit. The new heater goes in with new connectors and any code-required items, such as a pressure relief valve and discharge pipe. Gas units are checked for venting and leaks, electric units for a correct electrical connection.

Before the plumber leaves, the heater is filled, tested for leaks and brought up to temperature. You get a walkthrough of the temperature setting and the shutoff valves so you know where they are next time.

The Permit Step in Rolling Meadows

The City of Rolling Meadows states on its FAQ that a permit is required for a new or replacement water heater. The permit comes through the Community Development Department at 3600 Kirchoff Road, and the city requires contractor licenses and bonds on file before it issues a permit.

That means a replacement is not just a swap. It includes the permit and the chance of an inspection. Planning for that step avoids problems when you later sell the house, because an unpermitted heater can come up in a buyer's inspection. Townhome owners on the west side can also see plumber in the Plum Grove area.

What the City Says About Venting and Supply Pipe

The city's plumbing FAQ gives a few specifics that matter for a replacement. Single-wall vent pipe needs at least six inches of clearance from combustibles, B-vent needs one inch, and water supply pipe in copper or galvanized must be type M or heavier. Plastic pipe is commonly SDR26, though the code allows other types.

These are the kinds of details a plumber checks when connecting a new heater, and they are why an old installation sometimes has to be corrected, not just copied, when the new unit goes in.

Why Replacement Beats Repair in Some Cases

If the tank is rusted, the unit is old or the repair would cost close to a new heater, replacement is the better value. Repairing a worn tank can mean paying twice, once for the part and again when the tank fails.

The plumber can show you the condition of the tank and the data label, and give you a clear price for both options if a repair is possible. You choose. See water heater repair for what can be fixed instead.
